PART 1: COMPREHENSIVE JUSTIFICATION OF SYSTEM CAPABILITIES IN MVP
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. IMAGE UPLOAD, SCANNING, AND QUALITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Image upload & scanning; Scanning & analyzing images of packaged commodities; Attachment of photographs & supporting evidence]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Multi-Modal Capture: Users can either upload high-resolution package images directly (JPG, PNG, WebP) or use the live camera capture interface featuring real-time framing guides for Principal Display Panels (PDP), side panels, manufacturer info, and gable top/MRP seals.
  2. Cryptographic Evidence Registration: Upon ingest, the backend (FastAPI) computes a deterministic SHA-256 hash for every image. If an identical hash is uploaded, the deduplication engine prevents redundant storage. Each image is stored as an immutable `EvidenceAsset` linked to an `InspectionCase`.
  3. Pre-Analysis Quality Gating: Before OCR and AI processing, the `ImageQualityService` evaluates:
     - Laplacing sharpness variance (detects camera blur / motion blur).
     - Exposure distribution (flags extreme underexposure < 35 or overexposure > 220).
     - Minimum resolution boundaries (ensures label dimensions >= 600x600 px).
     - Images are categorized as `USABLE`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, or `UNUSABLE`. Blurry or dark images trigger quality warnings so inspectors can re-capture prior to enforcement decisions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. OCR EXTRACTION & PROVENANCE-GROUNDED AI PERCEPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Automated extraction & validation; Extraction of declarations from packaging; Detecting mandatory declarations]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Local Deterministic OCR: CompliScan uses local PaddleOCR ONNX (DBNet text detection + SVTR text recognition) running on CPU/DirectML. It outputs an array of discrete text tokens with bounding polygons `[x1, y1, x2, y2]` and character confidence scores.
  2. Controlled LLM Semantic Structuring: The extracted OCR tokens are sent to `gemini-3.6-flash` through a hardened prompt schema. Gemini is restricted strictly to semantic structuring and entity mapping; it is prohibited from making legal compliance determinations.
  3. Strict Provenance Token Grounding: Every structured declaration domain (Manufacturer, Commodity, Net Quantity, Dates, MRP, Consumer Care, Origin) must supply exact integer `source_token_indices` pointing to the PaddleOCR token array.
  4. Anti-Hallucination & Phantom Token Rejection: The `ExtractionService.validate_provenance` validator verifies every index boundary at runtime. Any response referencing out-of-bounds tokens is rejected before database persistence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MULTI-IMAGE PRODUCT EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS (NO MAJORITY VOTING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Checking correctness, completeness across multi-panel labels; Multi-evidence consolidation]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Real packaged commodities spread mandatory declarations across 4-6 distinct surfaces (front, back, sides, top gable).
  2. The `ProductEvidenceSynthesisService` deterministically aggregates per-image declarations into a single unified `ProductDeclaration` model.
  3. Strict Conflict Detection: If different panels declare conflicting information (e.g. front panel says "Real Fruit Power Mixed Fruit Juice" and side panel says "Mixed Fruit Beverage", or differing MRPs), the engine flags `observation_status = CONFLICTING` and records `conflicting_evidence_ids`.
  4. Zero Heuristic Selection: CompliScan NEVER uses majority voting or averaging to guess truth. Conflicting evidence is surfaced explicitly for human reviewer adjudication.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. RULE-BASED LEGAL METROLOGY COMPLIANCE CHECKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Rule-based compliance checking; Identifying missing or non-compliant declarations; Detection of missing, misleading or non-standard declarations]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Zero Legal Decisions in Gemini: The compliance engine is written entirely in deterministic Python (`ComplianceEvaluationService`) based on Rule 6(1) of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
  2. The 7 Canonical Statutory Checks:
     - Rule 6(1)(a) [Manufacturer/Packer/Importer]: Validates presence of responsible entity name and complete physical address (premises, city, pin code).
     - Rule 6(1)(b) [Generic Commodity Name]: Checks for generic/common name; flags `REQUIRES_REVIEW` if conflicting names exist across panels.
     - Rule 6(1)(c) [Net Quantity & Standard Units]: Validates numeric quantity > 0 and legal metric unit (g, kg, ml, l, m, N, U). Flags non-standard units (e.g. "gms", "GM", "Kgs") as `POTENTIAL_NON_COMPLIANCE`.
     - Rule 6(1)(d) [Month & Year of Manufacture/Packing]: Validates valid 2-digit month (1-12) and 4-digit year.
     - Rule 6(1)(e) [MRP & Tax Inclusivity]: Validates retail sale price amount and enforces mandatory statutory phrasing ("inclusive of all taxes" or "incl. of all taxes"). Missing tax wording fails automatically.
     - Rule 6(1)(f) [Consumer Care Details]: Enforces at least one contact channel (Toll-free phone number, email address, postal address).
     - Rule 6(1)(da) [Country of Origin]: Evaluates origin context. If `DOMESTIC`, correctly marks `NOT_APPLICABLE` (exempt). If `IMPORTED`, enforces mandatory origin country declaration.
  3. Controlled Result Vocabulary: `PASS`, `POTENTIAL_NON_COMPLIANCE`, `REQUIRES_REVIEW`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `INCOMPLETE`, `PROCESSING_FAILED`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. FONT SIZE, PLACEMENT, AND READABILITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Font size and readability analysis; Checking readability and font size requirements; Checking placement of declarations]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Spatial Bounding Box Geometry: PaddleOCR ONNX extracts bounding polygon coordinates `[x1, y1, x2, y2]` and calculates text height relative to total packaging area and principal display panel (PDP) dimensions.
  2. Relative Height Validation: Calculates character height in millimeters based on image DPI and label physical dimensions, comparing against First Schedule thresholds under Legal Metrology Rules (e.g. minimum 2.0 mm for net weight > 500g/ml up to 4.0 mm for larger packs).
  3. Contrast & Exposure Ratio: Evaluates pixel intensity histograms in the local bounding box region of mandatory text against surrounding label background to verify minimum visual contrast and legibility under varying retail lighting conditions.
  4. Panel Placement Check: Verifies that net quantity and generic commodity name appear on the Principal Display Panel (PDP) rather than hidden folds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. INSPECTOR WORKFLOW, REVIEWER ADJUDICATION & SECURE RBAC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Role-based user access & secure authentication; Inspector verification & Reviewer decisions]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Supabase JWT Authentication: Secure authentication with encrypted tokens, bcrypt password hashing, and database-level role resolution (`INSPECTOR` vs `REVIEWER`).
  2. Inspector Role: Field officers capture packaging evidence, inspect automated rule findings, add operational notes, and submit cases for review (`INSPECTION_SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW`). Inspectors cannot finalize cases or override statutory findings.
  3. Reviewer Role: Senior adjudicating officers access a dedicated Review Queue (`get_review_queue`). Reviewers can confirm findings or override them with mandatory written justification rationales (minimum 5 characters).
  4. Evidence Requests: Reviewers can issue formal requests (`ER-xxxxx`) requiring inspectors to capture supplemental panels before final sign-off.
  5. Immutable Finalization: When the reviewer issues a final determination (`COMPLIANT`, `NON_COMPLIANCE_CONFIRMED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`), `FinalizationService` seals the case into a `FinalAuditRecord` and locks the inspection into `READ_ONLY` status (HTTP 409 prevents tampering).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. GENERATION OF DIGITAL COMPLIANCE REPORTS (PDF & DOCX PARITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Generating compliance reports & violation summaries; Generation of digital compliance reports in PDF and editable formats; Export of reports to PDF and editable formats]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Direct Snapshot Generation (Gate 6): Reports are generated strictly from the sealed `FinalAuditRecord` database snapshot. No dynamic LLM generation or runtime fabrication is permitted.
  2. Official Indian Departmental Format (5 Sections):
     - Section 1: Official Header (Govt. of NCT of Delhi / Legal Metrology Dept), Case Reference, Product Context, Origin Status, Inspector/Reviewer metadata.
     - Section 2: Evidence Register containing SHA-256 cryptographic hashes, file sizes, token counts, and image quality metrics.
     - Section 3: Multi-Angle Declaration Synthesis Table showing corroborating image counts and token lineage.
     - Section 4: Statutory Compliance Evaluation Ledger with Rule Citations (6(1)(a)-(da)), Automated Findings, Reviewer Determinations, and Officer Justification Rationales.
     - Section 5: Authenticated Final Determination Banner, Reviewing Officer Signature block, Cryptographic Audit Seal (SHA-256), and statutory legal notice under Section 15 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
  3. Dual Format Parity: 
     - PDF generated via ReportLab (`PDFReportService`) for unalterable court-ready enforcement submissions.
     - DOCX generated via Python-Docx (`DOCXReportService`) for editable departmental documentation with matching table borders, typography, and status callouts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. REPOSITORY, SEARCH, AND RETRIEVAL FACILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Maintaining a repository of scanned products & compliance history; Search and retrieval facility for previously scanned products and reports; Repository of scanned products and inspection history]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Central Inspection Ledger: Persisted in PostgreSQL database with relational indexing across `inspections`, `evidence_assets`, `compliance_findings`, `product_declarations`, and `final_audit_records`.
  2. Search & Retrieval API (`/api/v1/repository/search`):
     - Full-text search across Case Numbers, Product Names, Commodity Categories, and Manufacturer Names.
     - Advanced Multi-Criteria Filtering: Filter by statutory compliance result (`COMPLIANT`, `NON_COMPLIANT`, `REQUIRES_REVIEW`), date ranges, inspector ID, or origin status (`DOMESTIC`/`IMPORTED`).
     - SQL Injection Safe: Parameterized SQLAlchemy queries ensure total database safety.
     - Scoped Visibility: Inspectors view their own cases; Reviewers and Admins access full departmental repository records.
  3. Audit Event Timeline: Every action (creation, upload, OCR, extraction, evaluation, submission, override, finalization, download) is logged immutably in `audit_events`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. DASHBOARDS FOR ENFORCEMENT OFFICIALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Features Covered: Providing dashboards for enforcement officials; Dashboard for monitoring inspections, violations and product compliance details; Dashboard for monitoring compliance status and enforcement activities]

* How it Works in CompliScan LM MVP:
  1. Real-Time Metrics Aggregation (`/api/v1/dashboard/metrics`):
     - Total Cases Processed, Open Cases, Cases in Review, Finalized Inspections.
     - Violation Rate (%) and breakdown of top non-compliant rules (e.g. Rule 6(1)(e) MRP tax wording vs Rule 6(1)(a) address omission).
     - Breakdown by Commodity Category (Food, Beverages, Cosmetics, Electronics, Household).
  2. Review Queue Triage: Shows pending inspections with count of automated violations and open evidence requests (`ER-xxxxx`) for rapid prioritization.
  3. Visual Enforcement Timeline: Interactive chart displays inspection volume and violation frequency over daily, weekly, and monthly periods.

PART 3: SHORT EXECUTIVE NOTES ON EACH FEATURE

1. Package Image Scanning & Analysis:
   Captures packaging images via camera or file upload, validates sharpness and exposure before running OCR.

    Combines PaddleOCR ONNX with token-grounded Gemini 3.6 Flash extraction, enforcing strict boundary validation.
